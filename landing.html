FAQ

Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no server to pay for, so there's nothing to charge.

Does it watch me through the camera?

No. It notices when you leave the tab; you log the rest in one tap.

Where is my data?

Only in this browser. Clear it any time from Settings.

Can I use it as a Mac app?

Yes — in Safari, open Lockin and choose File → Add to Dock.
