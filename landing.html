    Is it really free?

    Yes. There's no server to pay for, so there's nothing to charge.

    How does it know I looked away?

    It notices when you leave the tab or window. The rest — phone, people, daydreams — you log in one tap.

    Can I use it as a Mac app?

    Yes. Open Lockin in Safari and choose File → Add to Dock.
